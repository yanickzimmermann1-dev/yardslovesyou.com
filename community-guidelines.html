Community Guidelines

The YARDSLOVESYOU Community is a place to share ideas, get inspired and connect with other producers. Keep it about the music and keep it respectful.

1. Be respectful

No insults, harassment, hate speech, threats or discrimination – in titles, bios or anywhere else.

2. Keep it clean

  • No sexual, violent or otherwise offensive titles, names or bios.
  • No spam, advertising or misleading links. Links in your profile should lead to your own music or channels.
  • Don't impersonate other artists, producers or YARDSLOVESYOU.

3. Remixing is welcome

Every beat and chord idea you publish can be played and remixed by others – that's the point. Remixes show a link to the original. By publishing, you allow other members to use your pattern as a starting point in the Beat Lab or the Chord Starter and in their own music.

4. Your account

  • You must be at least 16 years old.
  • One person, one account. New accounts can publish up to 3 posts per day.
  • You can delete your posts or your whole account at any time in your account settings.

5. Moderation

Use the Report button if something breaks these rules. We may hide posts or profiles that break the rules, and remove accounts that do so repeatedly. Questions? Write to yardsyurr@gmail.com.
