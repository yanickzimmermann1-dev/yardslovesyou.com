FAQ

Questions

When does it launch?

Recording starts this winter. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first.

Is it royalty-free?

Yes. Use the sounds in your own music, commercial or not. You can't resell or share the sounds themselves. Full terms are on the licence page.

Do I need special software?

No. Files are 24-bit WAV and work in any DAW or sampler.

What's in the Raw Field Recordings add-on?

The unprocessed recordings the sounds were made from, tagged with place and date, so you can build your own.
