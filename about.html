ABOUT

Hi, I'm yards.

I'm a producer and a mixing & mastering engineer from Switzerland, working with hip-hop and trap artists online. I mix and master your tracks, and I make sound kits and guides for producers.

STORY

How it started

It started with the guitar about 15 years ago. Two years later I moved into a DAW and began producing. Famous Dex, EarthGang and A$AP Rocky were early inspirations.

I then studied audio engineering with a focus on music production and graduated with a diploma. For 5 years now I've been mixing and mastering for artists.

SOUND

Balance and experimentation

These two matter most to me. I want a mix that is balanced and translates everywhere, while leaving room to try something new.

HOW I WORK

Listening first

Communication and listening are key. I start with your references and your vision.

Sessions can happen in person wherever you want. I'm happy to come to you. I speak German, English and French, and I'm based in Switzerland, working online worldwide.
