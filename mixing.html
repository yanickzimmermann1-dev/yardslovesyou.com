Questions

Good to know

Which file format should I send?

For Mastering, upload one WAV file for a single song, or one ZIP with all your songs. For stem-based services, put your stems into one ZIP. As a recommendation: export WAV at 24-bit, keep the same sample rate for every file, start all stems at the same point (bar 1), leave the master limiter off your stems, and name your files clearly.

How many stems can I send?

Stem Mastering takes up to 8 stems per song. Mixing and Mix + Master take up to 30 stems per song. Put them all into one ZIP file.

How long does it take?

Mastering takes 3 business days, Stem Mastering 5 business days, and Mixing and Mix + Master 7 business days. If you are in a hurry, the Rush 48h add-on (+30%) gets you delivery within 48 hours instead.

How do revisions work?

Mastering and Stem Mastering include 1 revision. Mixing and Mix + Master include 3 revisions. Once I have delivered, you request a revision on your order status page.

What do I get back?

Mastering and Stem Mastering include WAV + MP3. I send you the result by email, and you can also follow your order on its status page.

Can I send several songs or an EP?

Yes. Put all your songs into one ZIP and set the number of songs in the form. From 4 songs you get a 10% EP discount, applied automatically.

How do I pay?

Checkout is secure via Stripe, and you can pay with TWINT, PayPal or card. Prices are per song and you always pay in CHF. The USD and EUR amounts shown are approximate hints only.

Can I follow my order?

Yes. After checkout you get an email with a link to your order status page, where you can follow your order and request revisions.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Use your included revisions first. If you are still not happy after the final delivery, contact me within 7 days and I will refund 50 % of that song's service price. See the Terms.
