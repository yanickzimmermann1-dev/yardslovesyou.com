Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains which personal data is processed when you use yardslovesyou.com, in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Controller

Yanick Zimmermann, YARDSLOVESYOU
Unterführungsstrasse 29, 4600 Olten, Switzerland
Email: yardsyurr@gmail.com

2. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit the site, technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of access is processed in server logs to deliver the website and keep it secure.

3. Fonts and scripts from third parties

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA) and program libraries from the jsDelivr network. Your browser connects to these servers, which transmits your IP address.

4. Track submissions

When you book a service, we process your name or artist name, your email address, your notes and the files you upload. We use this data only to carry out your order and to contact you about it. The data is stored with Supabase (Supabase Inc.) in the EU (Ireland).

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland, and Stripe Inc., USA). Your payment details are entered directly on Stripe's pages; we never see your full card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies: stripe.com/privacy.

6. Purchases and downloads

When you buy a digital product, we process your email address, the product purchased and the payment status, to provide your download and send you a confirmation. Emails are sent via Resend (Resend Inc., USA).

7. Free downloads, Mix Check and email updates

Free downloads and Sound Drop: When you request a free download (e.g. Essentials Lite) or sign up for the monthly Sound Drop, we process your email address to send you the download link. If you sign up for the Sound Drop or tick the box for email updates, we also use your address to send you the monthly Sound Drop, new releases and deals.

Mix Check: The audio analysis runs entirely in your browser. Your audio file is not uploaded or stored. If you request the full report, we receive the analysis results (measured values, no audio) and the file name, and use them only to create and send your report by email. They are not stored.

Email updates are voluntary. Every email contains an unsubscribe link, and you can also withdraw your consent by replying to any of our emails or by writing to the email address above. We store your email address, how you signed up, whether and when you gave consent, and when you unsubscribed. The data is stored with Supabase, and emails are sent via Resend (Resend Inc., USA).

8. Community accounts

If you create a Community account, we process your email address to send you login links, and the profile information you choose to publish: your handle, display name, bio and links to your music channels. Your profile, your posts (titles and the musical patterns you publish), your likes and who you follow are publicly visible. Reports you submit are only visible to us. You can delete individual posts or your whole account at any time in your account settings; this permanently removes your profile, posts, likes and follows. Login emails are sent via Resend (Resend Inc., USA), the data is stored with Supabase.

9. Embedded media

Product pages may contain videos from YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode) or Vimeo. Data is only transmitted to these services when such a page is opened or the video is played.

10. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Stripe may set cookies that are necessary for a secure checkout.

11. Disclosure abroad

Some of the services named above process data in the USA or the EU. Where required, the transfer is based on the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

12. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as required by law (e.g. accounting records). Uploaded files are deleted 6 months after the order has been completed.

13. Your rights

You have the right to request information about your personal data, and to have it corrected or deleted. Send your request to the email address above. You may also file a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
